The challenge · Intelligent Well Performance Analysis

One well. Three parts. One tool.

PART 01

Virtual flow metering

Estimates oil, gas and water rates from readily available sensor measurements.

Your task
Use well-test data to build a model that predicts daily oil, gas and water rates from high-frequency data.
PART 02

Pressure transient analysis

Estimates key reservoir characteristics from the pressure recovery after a well is shut in following production.

Your task
Use the VFM oil rate with pressure and lab data to analyse the build-up test, create diagnostic plots and obtain reservoir properties.
PART 03

Interactive application

A single tool that combines VFM and PTA for engineers and managers.

Your task
Let users input well data and get production predictions, reservoir-property estimates and diagnostic plots.
