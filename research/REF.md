# Research references

Links collected for the pitch (competition and background), 2026-11-21.
"Not read" means the page blocked automated access, so the description comes
from its title only.

## Closest to our product: production analysis software

### iFactory: AI-powered well performance dashboard
https://ifactoryapp.com/industries/oil-and-gas/ai-powered-well-performance-dashboard-every-well-metric-one-view
Marketing article for a dashboard that puts rate, water cut, GOR, ESP and rod-pump
health and chemical injection into one AI-scored view per well. Connects to
existing SCADA with no new hardware. No VFM or PTA mentioned.

### SLB OFM: well and reservoir analysis software
https://www.slb.com/products-and-services/delivering-digital-at-scale/software/ofm
Long-standing SLB tool for engineers to analyse production and reservoir data,
with custom workflows and data connectors. Production surveillance; the page
does not mention rate estimation, VFM or PTA.

### SLB: digital workflows for intelligent completions
https://www.slb.com/products-and-services/innovating-in-oil-and-gas/completions/well-completions/digital-solutions/production-optimization-digital-workflows-for-intelligent-completions
Software that consolidates sensor data from smart completions and recommends
downhole valve settings for cleanup and production. It only applies to wells
fitted with intelligent completions.

### QRI SpeedWise Galaxy
https://www.qrigroup.com/SpeedWise
Suite of AI apps for reservoir management and field planning: production
forecasting, decline curves, drilling, artificial lift. Planning-level work,
not daily rate estimation or well-test interpretation.

## Smart-completion hardware ("intelligent well" in the industry sense)

### Baker Hughes: well monitoring and analytics
https://www.bakerhughes.com/completions/well-monitoring-and-analytics
Permanent downhole gauges (fibre-optic and electronic) for pressure,
temperature, strain, acoustics and multi-zone flow. Hardware that produces
data, rather than software that interprets it.

### Halliburton: intelligent completions
https://www.halliburton.com/en/completions/well-completions/intelligent-completions
Downhole systems for real-time monitoring and remote flow control across
several reservoir zones. Hardware.

### SLB: well completions software
https://www.slb.com/products-and-services/innovating-in-oil-and-gas/completions/well-completions/digital-solutions
Overview of SLB's completion design and optimisation software, including the
surface control system for intelligent completions and Petrel completion
optimisation.

## Off-target (different problem)

### IPT Global: AI analytics for well integrity
https://iptglobal.com/building-smarter-wells-ai-driven-analytics-for-well-integrity-across-the-lifecycle/
Article on AI for well-barrier integrity, from drilling through to plug and
abandonment. Promotes the SureTec platform. About integrity, not production.

### Geoactive Interactive Petrophysics (IP)
https://www.geoactive.com/interactive-petrophysics
Well-log interpretation software: porosity, saturation, NMR, geomechanics.
Petrophysics, not production.

### H&P: Advanced Well Engineering (DrillScan)
https://www.hpinc.com/technologies/advanced-well-engineering
Drilling engineering service: vibration, torque and drag, casing wear,
real-time drilling advisory.

### Intelie 4.0: rigs and wells performance
https://intelie.com/drilling-and-performance
Real-time analytics for drilling and completions: rig-state detection, KPI
benchmarking, frac-stage analytics.

### WellPro Software
https://www.wellpro-software.com/
Site no longer resolves (domain dead). Nothing to read.
